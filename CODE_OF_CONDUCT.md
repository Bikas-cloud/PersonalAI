\# Code of Conduct



\## Our Commitment



The PersonalAI project is committed to maintaining a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who participates in the community.



We welcome contributions and participation regardless of experience level, technical background, nationality, language, gender, disability, or personal identity.



Our goal is to make collaboration productive, respectful, and accessible to all.



\## Expected Behavior



All community members are expected to:



\- Treat other participants with respect and consideration.

\- Communicate politely and constructively.

\- Be patient with contributors who have different experience levels.

\- Accept constructive feedback and differing viewpoints professionally.

\- Focus technical discussions on ideas, implementations, and evidence rather than individuals.

\- Give credit to the original authors of code, ideas, and resources.

\- Respect the privacy and boundaries of other participants.



\## Unacceptable Behavior



The following behaviors are not acceptable:



\- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.

\- Discriminatory language or behavior.

\- Insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive communication.

\- Publishing someone's private or personal information without permission.

\- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.

\- Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or collaboration.

\- Any other behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.



\## Scope



This Code of Conduct applies to all PersonalAI community spaces, including:



\- GitHub Issues and Pull Requests.

\- Code reviews and repository discussions.

\- Project-related communication and community activities.

\- Any situation where an individual is officially representing the PersonalAI project.



\## Reporting an Incident



If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the project maintainer.



You can find the maintainer's contact information through the GitHub profile:



https://github.com/Bikas-cloud



Please do not publish sensitive incident details in public GitHub Issues or Pull Requests.



If a private contact method is not available on the profile, you may open an Issue requesting a private reporting channel. Do not include personal or incident details in that public Issue.



Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.



\## Enforcement Responsibilities



The project maintainer is responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate responses.



The maintainer will consider the nature, severity, context, and impact of each reported incident.



Possible enforcement actions include:



1\. \*\*Warning:\*\* A private warning requesting that the behavior stop.

2\. \*\*Temporary Restriction:\*\* Temporary suspension of participation in project spaces.

3\. \*\*Permanent Removal:\*\* Removal or restriction of access to project spaces for serious or repeated violations.



Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, consistent, and focused on maintaining a respectful community.



\## Appeals



Anyone who believes an enforcement decision was made unfairly may contact the project maintainer privately and request a review.



Appeals will be considered based on the available information and circumstances.



\## Attribution



This Code of Conduct was prepared specifically for the PersonalAI project. It reflects common open-source community standards and expectations for respectful collaboration.
